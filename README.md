# Centre de Football Moussa Kandjoura Diarra (CFMKD) 🇲🇱⚽

Official website for **Centre de Football Moussa Kandjoura Diarra**, a youth football academy dedicated to scouting, training, and developing young talents in Mali.

![Centre de Football Moussa Kandjoura Diarra Badge](assets/images/logo.jpg)

## 🌟 Overview

- **Academy Name**: Centre de Football Moussa Kandjoura Diarra
- **Location**: Mali, West Africa
- **Emblem**: 3 Gold Stars, Twin Royal Blue Lions, Trophy Pedestal Football
- **Colors**: Royal Blue & White (Home), Neon Green & Black (Match), Training Gold & Black
- **Categories**: U13, U15, U17, U20 / Élite
- **Live Website**: [https://alimire.github.io/cf-moussa-kandjoura-diarra/](https://alimire.github.io/cf-moussa-kandjoura-diarra/)

---

## ⚡ Features

- 🌐 **Bilingual (French / English)**: Real-time dynamic language switcher for international scouts, partners, and local families.
- 📸 **Interactive Photo Gallery**: Filterable gallery (Squads, Matches, Training, Crest) with a full-screen Lightbox viewer.
- 🛡️ **Squad & Position Showcase**: Goalkeepers, Defenders, Midfielders, and Forwards breakdown with jersey numbers and tactical roles.
- 🎯 **Grassroots Philosophy & Pillars**: High-performance technical training, character education, academic tracking, and pro trial pathways.
- 🤝 **Support & Sponsorship Hub**: Clear ways for sponsors, donors, and pro clubs to contribute equipment and scout talent.
- 💬 **WhatsApp Direct Integration**: Instant messaging integration with pre-formatted registration and inquiry templates.
- 🚀 **GitHub Pages Deployment**: Automated continuous deployment with GitHub Actions.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5**: Semantic, accessible structure with SEO & Open Graph meta tags.
- **CSS3**: Custom athletic theme, glassmorphism, responsive grids, micro-animations.
- **JavaScript (Vanilla)**: Internationalization engine, Lightbox, animated stats counters, mobile drawer navigation.
